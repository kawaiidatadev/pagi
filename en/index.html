    Principles

    How we decide, even when it doesn't suit us.

    Process before technologyWe don't propose a tool before we understand where the problem is.
    Measure to proveEvery project has a baseline and a measured result. If something can't be measured, we say so.
    Deliver it completeA solution isn't finished without documentation, a manual and training.
    SimplicityWe pick the simplest solution that solves the problem, even if it is a well-built spreadsheet.
    HonestyIf a project isn't right for you or we can't do it, we tell you before charging.
    ConfidentialityYour information is used only for your project and protected as our own.
    What we don't do
    • Sell a specific technology or licenses on commission.
    • Take projects without an identifiable, measurable problem.
    • Deliver solutions without documentation.
    • Reuse one client's information in another client's project.
    • Promise savings we can't measure.

    FAQ

    Before you write to us.

    Do I have to change systems or buy licenses?

    No. We work with what you already use: Excel, your ERP, your databases or your email. We only recommend a new tool if the assessment shows it is needed, and we take no commission on licenses.

    Do I get the source code?

    Yes. In automation projects we deliver the working solution, the project's source code, documentation and training. Ownership passes to your company once the project is paid in full. The exact scope is set in writing in each proposal.

    What happens to my company's information?

    It is used only for your project and returned or deleted at the end. We can sign a non-disclosure agreement before reviewing sensitive processes or data.

    How does payment work?

    Fixed-scope project price: 50% upfront to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly support is billed monthly. Published prices are plus VAT.

    My company is small. Is it worth it?

    If a task takes several hours a week, probably yes. Use the calculator with your numbers; if the annual cost is lower than the project, we will tell you.
