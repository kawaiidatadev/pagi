    How it is calculated
    • hours per year = hours per week × people × weeks
    • current cost = hours per year × cost per hour
    • freed hours = hours per year × reduction
    • freed value = freed hours × cost per hour (value of time, not money saved)
    • automated cost = (hours per year − freed hours) × cost per hour + recurring cost
    • net benefit = (freed value − recurring cost) × years − investment
    • payback = investment ÷ monthly benefit (only if the monthly benefit is positive)
    • ROI = net benefit ÷ (investment + recurring cost × years)
    • Scenarios: the chosen reduction × 0.6 (conservative), × 1 (middle) and × 1.3 capped at 95% (optimistic).

      FAQ

      Before you write to us.

      Do I have to change systems or buy licenses?

      No. We work with what you already use: Excel, your ERP, your databases or your email. We only recommend a new tool if the assessment shows it is needed, and we take no commission on licenses.

      Do I get the source code?

      Yes. In automation projects we deliver the working solution, the project's source code, documentation and training. Ownership passes to your company once the project is paid in full. The exact scope is set in writing in each proposal.

      Do you use artificial intelligence?

      Only when it adds something measurable, for example to classify free text. We don't load confidential information into AI tools that keep it or train on it without your permission.

      What happens to my company's information?

      It is used only for your project and returned or deleted at the end. We can sign a non-disclosure agreement before reviewing sensitive processes or data.

      How does payment work?

      Fixed-scope project price: 50% upfront to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly support is billed monthly. Published prices are plus VAT.

      My company is small. Is it worth it?

      If a task takes several hours a week, probably yes. Use the calculator with your numbers; if the benefit doesn't cover the investment in a reasonable time, we will tell you.
